**The gap:** an honest third-party review by a credentialed jerky expert (Cole Mercer — pitmaster) that:

- Isn't Amazon/Walmart trying to sell you the product
- Isn't a Reddit user with 3 upvotes calling it "trash"

**Risk:** KD 34, and slots 1/2/5 are all brand-owned oldtrapper.com pages. Realistically we can compete for slots 3-10 (the non-brand slots). **Ethical framing note:** This is a critical review, not a hit piece. Cole should be honest about what Old Trapper is (mass-market brand, widely available, decent value) and where it falls short (soft texture, high sodium, added sugars). Named entities only. Zero fabricated ratings, prices, or comparisons.
