# Is Beef Jerky Healthy? Nutrition Facts

Homemade beef jerky is a healthy high-protein snack -- a 1-oz serving delivers 12-15 g of protein with just 1-3 g of fat and 70-110 calories. The main nutritional concern is sodium, which can run 300-600 mg per serving depending on the recipe. Lean cuts, low-sodium soy sauce, and minimal added sugar make it one of the best portable protein sources available.

## Is Beef Jerky Good for Weight Loss?

Beef jerky can support weight loss due to its high protein-to-calorie ratio. Protein promotes satiety -- it keeps you full longer than carbohydrates or fat. However, the high sodium content can cause water retention that temporarily masks fat loss on the scale. Stick to 1-2 oz portions and choose plain or low-sodium recipes.

## Beef Jerky on a Keto Diet

Beef jerky is one of the most carnivore-friendly keto snacks because it's almost pure protein and fat with minimal carbs. To make **keto beef jerky**:

1. Replace soy sauce with coconut aminos
2. Skip all sweeteners (brown sugar, honey, maple syrup)
3. Season with garlic powder, smoked paprika, black pepper, and cayenne
4. Use the fattiest acceptable cut (flank or brisket) if you're targeting higher fat macros

A plain keto jerky recipe contains approximately 0-1 g net carbs per oz serving.

## Is Beef Jerky Good for a Carnivore Diet?

Yes -- plain beef jerky made with only salt, pepper, and garlic is completely carnivore-compatible. It's the ultimate portable carnivore snack: concentrated beef protein with zero plants. Make it in bulk batches, vacuum seal, and it keeps for months.

## Healthy Beef Jerky Recipe Tips

- **Use lean cuts** -- eye of round, top round, and flank steak have less than 5 g fat per oz
- **Low-sodium soy sauce** cuts sodium by 40% without changing flavor significantly
- **Skip sugar** -- plain and peppered jerky can be made with zero added sugar
- **Portion control** -- 1 oz is one serving; it's easy to eat 3-4 oz in one sitting
