# How Long Does Beef Jerky Last? Storage Guide

Beef jerky shelf life depends on three things: whether it's commercial or homemade, how it's stored, and whether curing salt was used. Commercial beef jerky lasts 1-2 years unopened and 1-2 weeks after opening. Properly made homemade beef jerky lasts 1-2 weeks at room temperature, up to 1 month refrigerated, and 3-6 months frozen.

## Beef Jerky Shelf Life by Storage Method

| Storage Method | Commercial Jerky | Homemade Jerky |
|---|---|---|
| Room temp, sealed, unopened | 1-2 years | 1-2 weeks |
| Room temp, airtight, opened | 1-2 weeks | 1-2 weeks |
| Refrigerator, airtight | 1-3 months | 1-3 months |
| Freezer, vacuum-sealed | 1 year+ | 3-6 months |

## How Long Does Homemade Beef Jerky Last?

Homemade beef jerky has a shorter shelf life than commercial jerky because it lacks the artificial preservatives found in commercial products. The shelf life depends on:

1. **Curing salt** -- Homemade jerky made with Prague Powder No. 1 (sodium nitrite) can safely last 2 months at room temperature when vacuum-sealed. Without it, 1-2 weeks.
2. **Fat content** -- Lean cuts (eye of round, top round) last longer. Fatty cuts go rancid faster.
3. **Moisture content** -- Properly dried jerky with moisture below 35% lasts much longer than under-dried, soft jerky.
4. **Storage container** -- Vacuum-sealed bags dramatically outperform zip-lock bags.
5. **Humidity** -- High-humidity climates shorten shelf life; always refrigerate in summer.

## How to Preserve Beef Jerky Longer

### Vacuum Sealing
Vacuum sealing removes oxygen, which is required for bacterial growth and oxidation (the process that makes fat go rancid). A vacuum sealer is the single best investment for home jerky makers who batch-produce and store.

### Oxygen Absorbers
Add oxygen absorber packets to mason jars or zip-lock bags. One 100cc packet is sufficient for up to 1 lb of jerky. This is the next best option after vacuum sealing.

### Curing Salt
Adding Prague Powder No. 1 at 1/4 teaspoon per 5 lbs of meat provides nitrite-based preservation that significantly extends shelf life and adds safety against bacterial growth.

## How Long Does Beef Jerky Last Unrefrigerated?

Commercial beef jerky can sit unrefrigerated in its unopened package for 1-2 years. Once opened, 1-2 weeks at room temperature is safe if the bag is resealed properly. Homemade jerky: 1-2 weeks maximum at room temperature in an airtight container -- shorter in warm or humid conditions.

## How to Tell If Beef Jerky Has Gone Bad

Signs that beef jerky has spoiled and should be discarded:

- **Sour or rancid smell** -- any smell other than smoky, savory, or slightly salty is a warning sign
- **Soft, sticky, or wet texture** -- properly dried jerky should be dry and leathery
- **Visible mold** -- white, green, gray, or fuzzy spots anywhere on the surface
- **Off colors** -- beyond normal dark brown to black darkening
- **Metallic or chemical taste** -- indicates fat oxidation (rancidity)

## Can You Leave Beef Jerky Out Overnight?

Yes -- beef jerky can be left out overnight without spoiling, as long as it's in an airtight container and the environment isn't extremely hot or humid. For any period longer than a day, refrigerate it or return it to sealed storage.
