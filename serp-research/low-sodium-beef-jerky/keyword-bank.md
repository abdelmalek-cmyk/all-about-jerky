## Overrides against pure-volume ordering (H2 order in the pillar)

Per master-prompt Phase 1 rule: *"default to search-volume order, BUT override it when intent logic demands."*

- **"What Counts as Low Sodium Beef Jerky" (definition)** goes first even though it's tail volume — reader can't judge brands or make a recipe without knowing the threshold definition. Foundation move.
- **"How Much Sodium Is in Regular Beef Jerky" (180 combined)** goes SECOND — it's the "why does this matter" hook that makes the whole pillar relevant.
- **"Best Low Sodium Beef Jerky Brands" (buying — ~90+40 combined)** goes BEFORE the home recipe — commercial intent readers outnumber home-cooks here (competitive density 0.95 = high commercial interest). But recipe H2 goes right after so it doesn't feel like a pure buying guide.
- **"Healthiest Beef Jerky" (390 combined)** goes AFTER low-sodium brands — health intent is a subset of "which is best," so it logically follows the brand comparison rather than leading it.

Final H2 order: what-counts → how-much-sodium → why-jerky-is-salty → best-brands (comparison table) → how-to-make → healthiest-jerky → dry-brine-vs-wet → no-salt-variants → is-it-heart-healthy → where-to-buy → wrap.
