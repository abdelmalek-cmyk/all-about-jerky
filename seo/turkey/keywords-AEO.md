## Critical content requirements

**FOOD SAFETY — 165°F (same as chicken, higher than beef's 160°F).** Turkey is poultry: salmonella risk means the USDA pre-heat method (bring strips to 165°F **before** drying, since most home dehydrators can't reliably hit a pathogen-killing temperature) must lead the method section, not be a footnote. Cite **USDA FSIS**. This is the same differentiator that made the chicken pages strong.

**NUTRITION — cite, don't invent.** The low-sodium/health cluster is 2,060/mo, the page's second-biggest. Use **USDA FoodData Central** for real per-serving figures. Never fabricate numbers (red line).

**PREGNANCY — be careful and non-prescriptive.** State general food-safety facts and direct readers to their healthcare provider. No personalized medical advice.

**DOGS — plain turkey only.** No garlic, onion, salt, or seasoning (allium toxicity). Cite AKC/ASPCA. Link to `/chicken-jerky-for-dogs/`.

**BRANDS — do not review.** A large share of the raw 588 keywords are brand/retail terms (Jack Link's, Perky Jerky, Chomps, Costco, Trader Joe's, Kirkland). Those were stripped from this bank. Teach label-reading criteria instead of writing brand reviews or inventing product specs.
