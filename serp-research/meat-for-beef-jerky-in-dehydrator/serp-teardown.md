**Cannibalization mitigation (user chose Option A, we build):**

- Recipe is embedded but shorter than `/dehydrator-beef-jerky/`'s equivalent (link to that pillar for the deep how-to)
- Deep internal links flow **from** this pillar **to** both existing pillars — this positions this new page as the "hub" for the meat-in-dehydrator intent while the older pillars serve their original narrower intents
