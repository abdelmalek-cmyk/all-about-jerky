## 7. Winner-type + gap (the strategic call)

**Winner type: fragmented — three separate SERPs sharing a URL.** The ranking pages each solve ONE intent:
- LVJCO/PrimalSpirit → brand navigational
- TastingTable/Sporked/MakeItDairyFree → "best brands" browsing
- SarahsVeganKitchen → home cooking

- Amazon → buy-now

**No single page owns "vegan jerky" the concept.** That's the opening. A well-structured hybrid pillar that answers what-is + how-to-make + best-brands can leapfrog several of the specialists on breadth.

**Risk:** KD 32 is a real jump from `/jerky-gun/`'s KD 10. Domain authority matters more here — expect to land page 2 initially and climb over 3-6 months as internal linking + backlinks accrue. Not a same-week win. **The site's author is a beef pitmaster** — writing about vegan requires careful voice positioning (see outline proposal for the two options).
