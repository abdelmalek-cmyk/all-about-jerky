# Opinions Reference

Recurring beliefs and takes the writer holds, drawn from the samples. Use to keep stances consistent and to inform confident, opinionated angles.

## On writing & craft
- **Humor belongs in most writing** — it builds connection and makes copy memorable, and it's an underused art form.
- **Authenticity beats polish.** Being yourself (quirks and all) is the fastest way to build an audience.
- **Restraint wins.** Humor should be sprinkled, not slathered; less is more.
- **Failure is part of the process.** Even great comedians bomb; you can't know what resonates until you try.
- **Keep it inclusive and safe.** Avoid controversial/charged jokes; universal references travel further than niche ones.
- **Practice compounds.** Skill is like darts — accuracy comes with reps.

## On tools & workflow (from the LinkedIn posts)
- **Don't pay your way out of a limits problem.** Buying more credits is a trap ("generational debt"); fix the workflow instead.
- **Move work to where it's cheaper/less limited.** Build a design system, then export and work in the cheaper/less-limited tool.
- **Model choice matters.** Use the powerful model to build, the cheaper model to edit.
- **Efficiency is a skill.** Batch prompts, reuse a brand kit, use inline comments over chat, upload only what's needed, start fresh chats, exploit caching windows.
- **Modern AI design tools beat legacy stacks** for speed ("Figma and Adobe are having a very bad week"; WordPress = "hours of design gymnastics").
- **Teach what you learn the hard way.** Package hard-won lessons and share them.

## Tone of opinions
- Held confidently but not arrogantly; softened with humor and self-awareness.
- Backed by lived experience ("I learned the hard way," "I just built…").
- Framed as helpful guidance, not dogma.

## How to deploy in content
- Lead sections with a clear stance, then justify with experience or example.
- Use contrarian framing sparingly for hooks ("The solution wasn't paying more").
- Keep opinions on-topic and non-political; when a topic is sensitive, present fairly and drop the jokes.
