**The gap:** an honest third-party craft-brand review by a credentialed pitmaster (Cole Mercer) that:

- Isn't a Reddit user with unverifiable opinions

**Risk:** KD 23 is genuinely low. Slots 1/4/5/6 are all brand-owned. Realistically we can compete for slots 2, 7-10 (Amazon + retailer + Reddit territory). Getting to page 1 within 2-3 months is plausible; getting to slot 3-5 within 6 months is achievable if the review builds link authority.

**Ethical framing:** RF is a real craft brand with clean sourcing and a distinctive brand voice. Cole's review should recognize what they actually do well (pasture-raised, no additives, bold flavors, unique branding) and be honest about trade-offs (premium price, limited retail distribution, product availability inconsistency per the Fowl Capone "sold out" note in the JerkyBrands listing).
