# How to Tell When Beef Jerky Is Done

Beef jerky is done when it passes the bend test: bend a cooled strip at a 90° angle and it cracks or tears slightly without feeling soft, rubbery, or moist. Done jerky looks dark brown throughout, feels firm and dry, and has no glistening surface moisture. If it bends completely without cracking, it needs more time.

## The 4 Doneness Tests

### 1. The Bend Test (Most Reliable)
Remove a strip and let it cool to room temperature (2-3 minutes). Bend it sharply at a 90° angle:

| Result | What It Means |
|---|---|
| Cracks and bends, may tear slightly | Done -- properly dried |
| Bends fully without cracking, feels rubbery | Underdone -- needs more time |
| Snaps completely in half, very brittle | Overdone -- too dry |

### 2. The Touch Test
A done strip should feel **firm and leathery** -- not soft, not crunchy. Press the thickest part with your finger. Properly dried jerky has no give or springiness.

### 3. The Visual Test
- **Done:** Deep brown or black-brown, slightly matte surface, uniform color throughout
- **Underdone:** Lighter brown, surface may look slightly glossy or wet
- **Overdone:** Very dark (nearly black), extremely rigid, crumbles at edges

### 4. The Internal Inspection
Bend a strip until it cracks open. Inspect the cross-section at the bend:
- **Done:** Uniform dark brown color all the way through; no pink, red, or translucent areas
- **Underdone:** Lighter pink or translucent center; visible moisture

## Why Jerky Seems Done Hot But Isn't

This is the most common beginner mistake. **Hot jerky is always softer and more pliable than cooled jerky.** Always pull a strip, wait 2-3 minutes, and test at room temperature. What feels "almost done" hot will often feel perfectly done after cooling.

## Doneness by Method

### Dehydrator Jerky Doneness
At 160°F, expect 4-6 hours for 1/4-inch slices. Check at the 4-hour mark. Rotate trays (bottom to top) if your dehydrator doesn't circulate evenly.

### Oven Jerky Doneness
At 175°F with door propped open, expect 3-5 hours. Jerky on the outer edges of racks dries faster than the center. Check edge strips first; give center strips additional time.

### Smoker Jerky Doneness
Smoker jerky takes 4-6 hours at 160-175°F. The smoke can make jerky look done (dark exterior) before it is. Always do the bend test -- visual color is not reliable with smoked jerky.

### Air Fryer Jerky Doneness
Air fryer jerky at 160°F typically takes 2-4 hours. Check every 30 minutes after the 2-hour mark. Air fryers vary significantly in power and airflow.

## What to Do If Jerky Is Underdone

Simply return underdone strips to the dehydrator or oven and continue drying in 30-minute increments, checking each time. Partially dried jerky is safe to return to heat -- you haven't wasted the batch.

## What to Do If Jerky Is Overdone

Overdried jerky is crumbly, very hard, and has poor flavor. You cannot re-hydrate it back to ideal texture, but you can: crush it into jerky flakes/dust for seasoning, chop it into bits for trail mix, or soften pieces briefly in warm broth before eating.
