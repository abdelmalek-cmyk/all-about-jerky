# Can Dogs Eat Beef Jerky?

Plain, unseasoned beef jerky made specifically for dogs is safe in small quantities. However, **store-bought and human-recipe beef jerky is unsafe for dogs** -- the combination of high sodium, garlic, and onion found in virtually every commercial and homemade jerky recipe poses real health risks to dogs.

<div class="answer-box">
<p><strong>Short answer:</strong> Human beef jerky = no. Homemade plain dog-safe jerky = yes, in moderation. The difference is in the seasoning. Never give your dog jerky containing salt, garlic, onion, sugar, or any spices.</p>
</div>

## Why Human Beef Jerky Is Unsafe for Dogs

| Ingredient | Risk to Dogs |
|---|---|
| High sodium / salt | Sodium poisoning; excessive thirst, vomiting, seizures |
| Garlic | Hemolytic anemia (destroys red blood cells) -- toxic even in small amounts |
| Onion / onion powder | Same as garlic -- toxic; allium poisoning |
| Xylitol (some brands) | Extremely toxic; causes hypoglycemia and liver failure |
| Chili / capsaicin | GI upset, vomiting, diarrhea |
| Sugar | Not toxic but promotes obesity and dental problems |

Even jerky that doesn't seem that salty to humans can cause salt poisoning in a small dog. A single strip of teriyaki beef jerky can contain 200-300 mg of sodium -- a dangerous dose for a 10-lb dog.

## How to Make Homemade Dog-Safe Beef Jerky

### Simple Dog Jerky (Strips)

**Ingredients:**
- 1 lb lean beef (eye of round, sirloin tip -- very little fat)
- Optional: 1/4 cup low-sodium, no-onion, no-garlic chicken broth for flavor

**Instructions:**
1. Slice beef 1/4-inch thick. Trim all fat (fat can cause pancreatitis in dogs).
2. Soak strips in low-sodium broth for 1-2 hours (optional).
3. Pat completely dry. Lay on dehydrator trays.
4. Dehydrate at 160°F for 4-6 hours until completely dry and firm.
5. Store in refrigerator -- dog jerky has no preservatives and should be refrigerated.
6. Shelf life: 1 week refrigerated, 3 months frozen.

### Beef and Vegetable Jerky Sticks for Dogs

**Ingredients:**
- 1 lb extra-lean ground beef
- 1/2 cup sweet potato puree (plain, cooked, no additives)
- 1/4 cup carrot, finely grated
- 1 egg (binder)

**Instructions:**
1. Combine all ingredients in a bowl. Mix thoroughly.
2. Load into a jerky gun or piping bag. Pipe 4-inch strips onto dehydrator trays.
3. Dehydrate at 160°F for 5-7 hours until firm and completely dry.
4. Store refrigerated in airtight container, up to 1 week. Freeze up to 3 months.

## Joint Health Beef Jerky for Dogs

Dogs with joint issues may benefit from treats containing glucosamine-rich ingredients. You can add 1/4 teaspoon of plain glucosamine powder per batch to the ground beef mixture above. Always consult your vet before adding supplements to your dog's diet.

## How Much Dog Jerky Is Safe?

Dog jerky treats should make up no more than **10% of your dog's daily calories**. For a 20-lb dog eating 500 calories per day, that's about 50 calories from treats -- roughly 2-3 small dog jerky strips. Overfeeding any treat, even healthy ones, can cause weight gain.
