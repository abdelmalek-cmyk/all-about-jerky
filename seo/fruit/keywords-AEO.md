## IMPORTANT — read this before writing: the intent reality

This keyword set is dominated by ONE brand, "Solely" (a company that sells packaged organic fruit jerky), which accounts for ~72% of all search volume. We do NOT sell products, so we cannot serve pure buy-intent. The winning strategy is therefore NOT to chase the head term "fruit jerky" (KD 41, brand-and-shop dominated). Instead:

1. **Own the recipe/how-to intent** — small but genuinely ours: "how to make fruit jerky", "fruit jerky recipe", "homemade fruit jerky", "mango fruit jerky".
2. **Intercept the Solely researchers with honest answers** — "is solely fruit jerky healthy" is **KD 7** (the easiest keyword here), plus "how is solely fruit jerky made", "how does solely make their fruit jerky". People researching the brand want to know if it's healthy and how it's made — answer both honestly (it's just pureed fruit dried into strips), then show them how to make the same thing at home for a fraction of the price. This converts brand-researchers into recipe-readers.
3. **Win the flavor long-tail** — mango (710/mo across variants) and pineapple are the biggest fruit-specific clusters.

Set expectations: this page targets a few hundred servable searches, not thousands. It's a reasonable content addition and a plant-based angle, but it will not drive the traffic the meat pages can. Treat it as breadth, not a volume play.

## Critical content requirements

**NO MEAT SAFETY ISSUE — this is the easy part.** Fruit jerky has no salmonella/pathogen concern, so no 160°F/165°F pre-heat. Dry fruit at ~135°F (57°C). This is genuinely simpler and safer than the meat pages — say so; it's a selling point for beginners and families.

**HONESTY ON "HEALTHY."** Don't overclaim. Fruit jerky/Solely is a whole-food snack (just fruit), which is genuinely good — BUT drying concentrates sugar and calories per bite, so it's not "eat unlimited." Give the balanced, USDA-sourced picture. This honesty is exactly what wins the KD-7 "is it healthy" query.

**BRAND HANDLING — Solely.** We're not reviewing or selling Solely. We're answering what people genuinely ask (is it healthy, how is it made) and teaching the DIY version. Don't invent Solely's exact recipe or nutrition specs — describe the general process (pureed organic fruit, dried) and cite the brand's own public info only if referenced generally. Never fabricate product claims.

**PLANT-BASED ANGLE.** This page opens the site to vegan/vegetarian/kids-snack audiences. Naturally use "vegan", "no sugar added", "whole food", "kid-friendly snack" where accurate — these are real secondary search angles and differentiate from the meat catalog.

**POSITION IT IN THE SITE.** This is the first non-meat page. In nav, it likely fits under a broader grouping (maybe its own "Fruit Jerky" entry, or under Recipes) rather than Meat & Cut. Link it to the dehydrator guide and — via the fruit-flavored-beef section — to the beef pages, so it's not orphaned.
