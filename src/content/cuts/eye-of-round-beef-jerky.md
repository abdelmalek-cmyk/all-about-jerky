Eye of round is the gold standard for homemade beef jerky. Professional jerky makers, commercial producers, and experienced home cooks all point to the same cut: a cylindrical roast from the rear leg of the cow that happens to be nearly perfect for dehydration.

**Why it works so well:** Eye of round contains less than 5% fat, which means it dries cleanly without going rancid, slices into uniform strips from a single cut direction, and absorbs marinade evenly across the entire surface. It's also one of the least expensive cuts at most butcher counters — typically $5–8/lb compared to $12–18/lb for flank or skirt steak.

## How to Identify Eye of Round at the Butcher Counter

Eye of round is sold as a whole roast (2–5 lbs, cylindrical) or pre-sliced. Look for:
- **Shape:** Cylinder, roughly 3–4 inches in diameter
- **Color:** Deep red, very little visible fat or marbling
- **Surface:** May have a thin layer of silverskin (white membrane) — remove before slicing

Some stores label it as "eye round roast" or "round eye roast." It comes from the round primal cut — the rear leg — which also produces top round and bottom round.

## Step-by-Step: Preparing Eye of Round for Jerky

![Beef sliced thin against the grain on cutting board showing muscle fiber cross sections](/images/beef-jerky-slicing.webp)

1. **Trim silverskin.** Use a flexible boning knife held flat against the roast. Slide under the white membrane and pull it away. Silverskin doesn't break down with marinating or heat — any that stays on will make the jerky tough and chewy.

2. **Trim fat.** Any remaining fat caps or pockets should be removed. Fat prevents marinades from penetrating, causes the finished jerky to feel greasy, and shortens shelf life from weeks to days.

3. **Semi-freeze.** Place the trimmed roast in the freezer for 45–60 minutes. This firms the meat so it holds its shape under the knife, producing clean, even slices rather than ragged tears.

4. **Slice.** For tender jerky, slice **across the grain** (perpendicular to the muscle fibers). For chewier jerky, slice **with the grain**. Target 1/4 inch (6mm) thickness. A mandoline or meat slicer set to 1/4 inch produces professional results.

5. **Marinate.** Eye of round's uniform surface absorbs marinade evenly. Because it's lean, marinating time is flexible: 8 hours minimum, 24 hours for maximum flavor penetration.

## Where to Buy

Any grocery store butcher counter carries eye of round, usually in the whole roast form. For sliced-to-order, ask the butcher to run it through the slicer at 1/4 inch — many will do this for free. Costco and Sam's Club sell large eye of round roasts (5–7 lbs) at significantly lower per-pound prices than standard grocery stores.
